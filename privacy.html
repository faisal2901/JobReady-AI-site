Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Your trust matters. This page explains how JobTopper handles your data.

  • Your resume is parsed in your browser and stored locally on your device unless you choose to use an AI tool.
  • When you use an AI tool, the relevant text is sent securely to our AI provider only to generate your requested result.
  • We keep basic account information, such as name and email, plus usage and referral counts needed to run credits and product analytics.
  • We do not sell your resume or personal data.
  • You can stop using the service at any time. Signing out keeps your local data on your device unless you clear browser storage.

Questions? Contact us through the WhatsApp or LinkedIn links in the app.
